## Experimental Alpha

Corrected Alpha packaging: includes the full MIT license and generated-audio terms notice inside the JAR. Gameplay source and assets are unchanged from the first Alpha.

Hytale 0.7 runtime: home-world nickel/oil shale, fueled native processing, finite propellant, full-size Odyssey/Kestrel rockets, joined launch-platform construction and the Lune route.

Compiled against pinned Hytale 0.7.3 APIs; representative local gameplay checks used 0.7.4. The declared range does not certify every future 0.7 patch. Requires Java 25.

**Known limits:** passenger movement, camera comfort and landing presentation remain under repair. Full survival acquisition, human visual/listening acceptance and actual multiplayer verification remain open. Later destinations, interiors and aerodynamic freeflight are not included. Use a separate backed-up test world, retain the matching world/player backup, and read the [development status](https://jkswoods.github.io/astral-reach/hytale0.7/status/).

With the game/server stopped, install this release's JAR in UserData/Mods, remove older Astral Reach JARs, and enable AstralReach in the world's Mods settings. No separate resource ZIP is needed.
