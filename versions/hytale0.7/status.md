# Development status {#development-status}

**Early development — some features may be incomplete or may not work as expected.** Alpha files are for experimentation and feedback. Back up complete world and player data before updating, and enable alpha versions in your launcher if needed.

## What you can play {#what-you-can-play}

Gather nickel and oil shale on Orbis, process materials and finite propellant, build a 25×25 launch platform, and choose from five large rockets. Flight Control funds 6,000 units outward and protects 3,000 for home; an earned expedition reward reduces those costs by 5%. Equip a complete suit and portable oxygen before boarding.

Lune is the playable landing destination. Explore craters, dunes, small rocks and lava tubes, mine titanium and aluminium, and return in the upper stage. New terrain and expedition sites appear in unexplored chunks. Existing builds and explored terrain remain intact.

A personal orbital station provides a protected linear concourse, nine external docking airlocks and starter platforms beside open building space. Solar arrays, batteries and life support support expansion. Invite builders, track station passes from Orbis, or prepare rescue equipment.

Lunar expeditions award a permanent fuel saving and paid recipes for a forty-minute oxygen tank and two-seat electric rover. Recover both records from one matching site; learned equipment still needs its normal materials.

## New controls and corrections {#new-controls-and-corrections}

The cheap Explorer Tablet is the Assembly Bench’s first recipe, under **Start here**. Its seven chapters explain the first launch and later equipment. Creative Supply can fill missing rocket parts and fuel without carried ingredients; Adventure remains paid.

Surface launch sites use the selected ship’s standing dimensions. The destination chart supports direct selection, station navigation and protected return fuel. Press Esc to hide it and use **/astral-map** to reopen it in orbit. Diffusers show their eight-block field with a cyan sphere; extinguished torches need breathable air or a powered oxygen field.

## Testing and limits {#testing-and-limits}

Local Hytale 0.7.5 checks cover paid tablet crafting, Creative rocket supplies, a complete Kestrel flight from a pad at Y128, station/destination navigation, docking and normal saved reloads. Some checks use prepared worlds, supplied equipment or private test fixtures; they do not establish an ordinary survival walkthrough.

- Human appearance, listening, sound levels and comfort acceptance remain open.
- Real multiplayer passenger, station-visitor and rescue flows need further testing.
- Full ordinary survival progression, every rocket/berth combination and interrupted docking cases remain unverified.
- Rocket and rover camera/body presentation can be rough. Rover Space braking remains unverified; leave space to coast.
- Later planet landings, walkable spacecraft cabins and aerodynamic freeflight remain development work. Lunar shelters and the rover cabin are unpressurised.
- The manifest supports **>=0.7.3 <0.8.0**, using pinned 0.7.3 APIs. This does not certify every later patch. Dedicated servers require Java 25.
