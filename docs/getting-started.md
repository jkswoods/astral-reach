# Your first expedition

Your first goal is a funded **Odyssey Heavy**, a complete launch site and a return trip with Lune's first upgrade materials. The entire bootstrap uses home-world resources.

## Gather and process

Enter `/astral-frontier` to create or load the persistent Orbis frontier. It grants no equipment. Gather stock iron, copper and processing fuel normally. Nickel and oil shale occur in new natural rock roughly **5–22 blocks below the local surface**.

Craft the stock Workbench, then the **Astral Assembly Bench** from 6 iron bars and 4 copper bars. Build its four [processors](machines.md). Smelt nickel, press plates and coils, refine oil shale and condition propellant cells. Keep outputs clear and feed each processor gathered fuel.

Including the bench, processors, Odyssey, complete launch platform, console and eight initial full cells requires **302 iron bars, 74 copper bars, 93 raw nickel and 16 oil shale**. Add tools, bootstrap equipment and processor fuel. See [recipes](recipes.md) for the exact parts.

## Raise a rocket

Build the [25×25 launch platform](launch-platform.md) at a low, open site. Place its separate edge console, select Odyssey and inspect the full-size cyan ghost. Supply 6 hull sections, 4 engine parts, 1 flight computer and 16 frame plates through **Feed needed materials**.

You may supply the plan in stages. When the pad and launch column are ready, the fully supplied rocket builds in 10 seconds. Completion fireworks celebrate the finished ship once. Its tank starts empty.

## Fund the voyage

Choose **Open flight control** at the console. Deposit eight full cells for **8,000 units**, retaining the returned casings. This pays for departure and protects 4,000 units for your journey home.

Choose **Prepare and launch round trip**. Flight preparation checks the arrival site before taking payment. The locally tested launch-platform build then shows a short departure and transfers you to Lune.

A separate flight prototype adds a longer third-person ascent and orbital map. Its longer ascent and orbital controls now have local gameplay checks; physical boarding and upper-stage landing remain under test. It is a different build from the established route described here. See [development status](status.md) before trying a supplied candidate.

## Bring the next voyage home

Mine titanium and aluminium under Lune's regolith, roughly **5–14 blocks deep**. The return beacon spends the protected reserve and restores your saved home departure point with your gathered inventory.

Smelt **12 titanium + 8 aluminium** into ingots, then press one **Thermal Shield**. That shield is part of the Kestrel Stack construction cost. Both current rockets serve the Lune route; later planet landings remain in development.

Read [Lune and progression](progression.md) before leaving home, and keep a complete backup while trying a development build.
